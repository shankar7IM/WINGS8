Our Mission

Your Computer. Everywhere.

Wings8 turns any screen into a personal computer. Three building blocks work together as one cohesive ecosystem: a sovereign operating system, a centralised platform to manage device fleets, and a family of affordable plug-and-play hardware.

One ecosystem, one goal: bring reliable, secure computing to the people and places the technology industry leaves behind.

  • W8 OS

    A sovereign operating system: ultra-light, high-performance and cyber-secured for minimal hardware.

  • W8 SaaS

    One unified platform to manage device fleets, W8 OS licences, remote backups, and invoicing.

  • 8Wares

    Plug-and-play hardware: W8 TV stick, W8 USB drive, Mini PCs, AR Glasses and a portable key in R&D.

W8 OS

A sovereign operating system

  • Ultra-light

    A minimal footprint that runs on the strictest minimum hardware configuration.

  • High-performance

    Engineered for speed and responsiveness, even on modest or ageing components.

  • Cyber-secured

    Sandboxed environments, no root access to system files, and hardware-bound licensing.

  • Sovereign by design

    Licence and data control, local administration, and reduced dependency on foreign operating systems.

OpenCore architecture

An open-source core with proprietary, privately licensed overlays built on top.

Sandboxing

Every environment and application is isolated. Reduced attack surface; no admin or root access to system files.

Hardware-bound licensing

Each licence is protected by an algorithm tied to the hardware. A blockchain-based version is on the roadmap.

Agentic AI OS

A dedicated AI layer with its own execution environment, running in parallel with the OS. R&D in progress.
